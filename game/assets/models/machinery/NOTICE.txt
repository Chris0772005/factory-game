zx200_hd.glb: "20 t class hydraulic excavator", modelled procedurally by the Construction Site Simulation
contributors (https://github.com/dtt-github/construction-site-simulation, assets/machinery/zx200_hd.glb),
Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt). The file is used unchanged; the game recolours and poses it at run time.

zx200_cab_open.glb: derived from the part "cab_shell" of zx200_hd.glb (Apache License 2.0, as above).
Changes: the closed block was hollowed out and window openings were cut behind its panes
(tools/blender/zx200_cab.py). The game shows it in place of the original part.

dump_truck.glb: based on "Dump Truck" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dump-truck-781c616da8f44982a59cb3fc3fa67f98)
by ElectroNick (https://sketchfab.com/ElectroNick), licensed under CC-BY-4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The unchanged source and its licence file are in
tools/blender/sources/dump_truck_electronick/. Changes (tools/blender/dump_truck.py): scaled to real size and
re-oriented, the six wheels separated into parts of their own, new materials and colours, clear panes, a lining
inside the cab, tail lamps and beacons added.
