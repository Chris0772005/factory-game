zx200_cab_open.glb: derived from the part "cab_shell" of zx200_hd.glb (Apache License 2.0, as above).
Changes: the closed block was hollowed out and window openings were cut behind its panes
(tools/blender/zx200_cab.py). The game shows it in place of the original part.

dump_truck.glb: based on "Dump Truck" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dump-truck-781c616da8f44982a59cb3fc3fa67f98)
by ElectroNick (https://sketchfab.com/ElectroNick), licensed under CC-BY-4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The source as it was fetched and its licence file are in
tools/blender/sources/dump_truck_electronick/. Changes (tools/blender/dump_truck.py): scaled to real size and
re-oriented, the six wheels separated into parts of their own, new materials and colours except on the wheels,
which keep the source's tyre-and-rim texture (M_dump_truck_E_01, base colour and normal, re-encoded as JPEG),
clear panes, a lining inside the cab, tail lamps and beacons added.

dump_truck_M_dump_truck_E_01_baseColor.jpg and dump_truck_M_dump_truck_E_01_normal.jpg are that texture, extracted
from dump_truck.glb by Godot's importer: same author and licence as dump_truck.glb. The other dump_truck_*.jpg
files are generated by tools/blender/dump_truck.py and are the game's own.
