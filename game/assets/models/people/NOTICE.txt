worker.glb, broker.glb, officer.glb, woman.glb, youth.glb: the townspeople of MOLTEN MATES. These five figures
are AI-GENERATED CONTENT. Nobody modelled or painted them by hand, and they show no real person: each face and
outfit was invented by the image model from a written description.

How they were made (all through the owner's Higgsfield account, https://higgsfield.ai, in November 2026):
  1. A studio picture of each person, front view in an A-pose, from a written description
     (Higgsfield image model "GPT Image 2.6").
  2. From that picture a textured, rigged 3D figure with one clip, a slow walk
     (Higgsfield 3D model "Meshy 7 image-to-3D": about 25,000 triangles, PBR maps, 24-joint skeleton,
     clip "Casual_Walk" of Meshy's animation library).
  3. For the first figure (worker) further clips of the same library on the same skeleton
     (Higgsfield "Meshy rigging"): "Quick_Walk", "Run_02", "Wave_One_Hand"; also "Idle" and
     "Confident_Walk", which are not used.
  4. tools/people.py put each figure together for the game: its own mesh, skeleton and slow walk (clip Stroll),
     the first figure's clips carried over to the others (Walk, Run, Wave), a clip of standing still made from the
     waving clip (Idle_Neutral), every clip set on the ground, and the maps laid out anew: the generator packs
     thousands of small patches edge to edge, whose colours run into each other from a few metres away; the
     tool moves the patches (each keeps its size and picture) so that like colours lie together with room
     between. Colour map 3072 px wide, normal and roughness maps half that, saved as JPEG files beside the models.

Import settings that matter (the *.import files beside the models): the maps are compressed for the graphics card
with mipmaps (compress/mode=2, the normal maps in high quality), and animation/remove_immutable_tracks is off,
so that every clip moves every bone and no pose is left over from the clip before.
