street_tree.glb: made from "Tree Small 02" (https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02) from Poly Haven, CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), in the prepared copy of the raisim2Lib project
(https://github.com/raisimTech/raisim2Lib, rsc/forest/tree_small_02 at commit
d9df2c979c9ddc44ec6b6727ff72c660688fdb62: one tree of the original line-up, 1K maps). Changes
(tools/blender/tree.py): of 30,000 leaf pieces one in eight is kept and enlarged, leaves, branches and trunk are
thinned to about 73,000 triangles in all, the black ground of the leaf map is filled with leaf colours, the tree is
resized to 6.2 m. The street_tree_*.jpg files are its maps, extracted from the GLB by Godot's importer.
