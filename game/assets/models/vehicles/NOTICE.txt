delivery_van.glb: based on "European Delivery Van"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/european-delivery-van-0b2f1ad95a79419f9a092420024d329c) by Evan Hiltz
(https://sketchfab.com/evan.hiltz), licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The source, its licence file and a note on its origin are in tools/blender/sources/european_delivery_van_hiltz/.
Changes (tools/blender/van.py): turned and centred, the 61 parts joined by material, the four wheels as parts of
their own, the maps re-encoded as JPEG and three of them reduced from 4096 to 2048 pixels. Shapes and painting are
the author's. In the game the panes are made clear, road dirt is laid over the body and the lamps are switched off.

The delivery_van_van_european_delivery_*.jpg files are that model's maps, extracted from delivery_van.glb by
Godot's importer: same author and licence as delivery_van.glb.

car_kiri86.glb, car_fairheaven_lt80.glb, car_fairheaven_sw84.glb: based on "Kiri '86 - Low poly model"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/kiri-86-low-poly-model-5c17c582ef9549798f694b660feea42a), "Fairheaven LT '80 - Low
poly model" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fairheaven-lt-80-low-poly-model-e2678da920cc4be68dbc193727919ffb) and
"Fairheaven SW '84 - Low poly model"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fairheaven-sw-84-low-poly-model-aa0becb6e854422596cac6b21bf79787) by Daniel
Zhabotinsky (https://sketchfab.com/DanielZhabotinsky), licensed under CC-BY-4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The sources, their licence files and a note on their origin are in
tools/blender/sources/zhabotinsky_cars/ (prepared copies from the raisim2Lib project: one mesh per car, 1K maps,
plain body paint). Changes (tools/blender/cars.py): turned upright and centred, split into body, inside and panes,
the panes given a clear material of their own, the badges left out. In the game each parked car gets its own paint
colour, a clear coat and road dirt.

The car_*_*.jpg and car_*_*.png files are those models' maps, extracted from the GLB files by Godot's importer: same
author and licence as the models.
