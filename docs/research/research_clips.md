## 1. Market context
- Steam released 21,382 games in 2025 and 13,359 by mid-July 2026 ([shanethegamer](https://www.shanethegamer.com/pc-mac/steams-retail-sim-flood-keeps-rising-as-2026-nears-2025s-full-year-total-already/)).
- **Next Fest only amplifies attention you already have.** In the February 2026 survey (182 devs), wishlists before the fest predicted wishlists gained during it with r=0.825. The median demo gained 806 wishlists; games starting under 1k gained a median of 322 ([presskit summary of HTMAG](https://presskit.gg/blog/steam-next-fest-2026-results), [HTMAG benchmarks](https://howtomarketagame.com/benchmarks/)). In June 2026, games under 1k wishlists were "drowned in the slop pile" ([HTMAG 2026-07-13](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/07/13/did-ai-slop-ruin-steam-next-fest-june-2026/)). So virality has to be built before the fests.
- **First-week sales:** median about 0.15× wishlists for games with more than 25k wishlists, and about 0.10× for games priced above $10 ([gamedevreports/GameDiscoverCo](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-the-state-of-steam)).
- **Short-form promos:** GameDiscoverCo Pro's 7-day wishlist list in summer 2026 noted "paid/organic shortform video promos based on viral concepts were hot" (KingFish #1, Meccha Chameleon #2) ([GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/june-2026s-top-new-pc-and-console)).
- **Friendslop is not saturated** (Zukowski, 2026-07-30). Combined friendslop player peak went from 280k (March 2025) to 366k (June 2026). A hit takes over 50% of that audience at launch and falls to about 10% within 3–9 months. Mage Arena sold 624k copies and made $3.69M in week one with only an 11.75% share ([HTMAG](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/07/30/is-friendslop-saturated/), [GWO](https://gameworldobserver.com/2026/08/03/analyst-there-are-many-games-in-the-friendslap-niche-but-it-is-not-oversaturated)).
- **Claims I don't trust:**
  - "TikTok organic reach collapsed in early 2025" ([fungies](https://fungies.io/indie-game-marketing/)/[respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-originals/the-indie-game-discovery-problem-is-getting-worse)) **[LOW]**.
  - "Short-form drives about 35% of indie wishlists" ([presskit](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/how-to-build-steam-wishlist)/[immutable](https://www.immutable.com/guides/steam-game-marketing)) **UNVERIFIED**, probably a vendor estimate.

## 6. Design rules for building virality into a "start tiny, grow huge" sim
1. **Title = premise, with a giant number.** A muted 3-second clip must explain itself (Needle, Digging A Hole, Meccha Chameleon).
2. **Capsule shows the premise at thumbnail size.** Be ready to mark it as the original (BitDot's red box).
3. **One-key "scale reveal" camera:** zoom out from one item to the whole mega-factory. Add a built-in before/after timelapse and a huge on-screen counter. These are ready-made Shorts. (Inference.)
4. **Rare, random, loud jackpot moments** with big VFX and sound (finding the needle, a legendary drop). This gives streams a "will it happen now" tension.
5. **Everything is a physics object.** Overflows, jams and spills of thousands of items should be funny, not punishing (R.E.P.O., How to Fish, RV There Yet?). Offer an optional high-stakes "risk it" choice for rage and reaction moments (gambling catches in How to Fish, Gamblers Table, Getting Over It).
6. **1–4 player online co-op with proximity voice,** and reasons to shout. Optionally use the mic as input, such as yelling commands at machines. I found no voice-controlled factory game, so this looks like an open gap (inference from YAPYAP and Mage Arena).
7. **Price $5–8 with a launch discount.** One friend buying should pull in the group (How to Fish's rationale, PEAK).
8. **Streamer tools:**
   - Viewer lobbies and join codes (as Meccha Chameleon did).
   - A streamer policy on the store page (as Meccha Chameleon did).
   - A DMCA-safe music toggle and chat-vote events or chat-named workers. These are UNVERIFIED best practice, not sourced here.
9. **Rhythm:** a payoff every 30–60 seconds, a tier jump every 5–10 minutes, and a 2–6h full game (Nodebuster about 3h, Digging Hole 1–2h), so a streamer can finish in 1–2 streams. A daily changing seed or map gives a reason to stream again (PEAK) ([gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/peak-why-this-co-op-indie-climbing-sensation-is-your-next-obsession)).
10. **Built-in clip capture:** a rolling 30-second replay buffer, vertical 9:16 export and photo mode. This follows Content Warning's in-game camera; it is not directly proven.
11. **Have the Steam page and a Playtest or demo live before the first viral post.** Turn views into sign-ups (Needle got 23k), because Next Fest gains follow the wishlists you bring in.
12. **Post many short premise tests** and measure wishlists, not views.
13. **Seed several mid-tier streamers in different regions,** and localize the game and store page.
14. **Moat against clones:** ship fast, own the name, and rely on depth or tech that a Roblox copy can't easily match, such as millions of simulated objects or a deep upgrade tree. This is inference.
