## D. Viral mechanics that keep recurring

1. **Low price ($3–10) plus 2–6 player co-op.** One friend buys, the whole group buys: Lethal Company, R.E.P.O., PEAK, Chained Together, Meccha Chameleon, How to Fish, RV There Yet?
2. **Players' own voices become content.** Proximity chat, mic as the input, voice mimicry: Lethal Company, R.E.P.O., Liar's Bar, Mage Arena, YAPYAP, Mimesis.
3. **Physics jank as a comedy machine.** Every run makes a new clip: R.E.P.O., PEAK, Chained Together, RV There Yet?, How to Fish.
4. **A premise you can read in one second.** It works as a thumbnail or a 3-second Short: 5 million pieces of hay, a painted body that is visible yet hidden, players chained together.
5. **Tiny teams and a 1–4 month jam origin.** PEAK, RV There Yet?, Meccha Chameleon, YapYap, and How to Fish (2 people).
6. **Streamer seeding is usually mid-tier and multi-region.** Mega streamers often arrive after the peak, as with Chained Together.
7. **Number-go-up reveals.** Balatro, TCG packs, Megabonk, Schedule I.
8. **Fantasy or taboo hook, or a parody of a big game.** Schedule I, Escape from Duckov.
9. **Mundane task taken absurdly literally.** PowerWash, the haystack game, the 2026 sorting craze.
10. **Rotating novelty or appointment drops.** PEAK's daily map, Dispatch's weekly episodes.
11. **One pre-launch clip on X or TikTok, or a demo, turns into wishlists.** The haystack game, YAPYAP, Balatro.

Audience behaviour: combined friendslop CCU was 280k in March 2025 and 366k in June 2026. A top title takes more than 50% of that audience at launch and falls to about 10% within 3–9 months. Zukowski's verdict is "not saturated", because the audience keeps moving to the next game ([HTMAG](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/07/30/is-friendslop-saturated/)).

- **Haystack games:** 10+ clones before the original has even launched.

- **Structural risk:** any simple social loop gets free Roblox clones within 48 hours to 3 weeks (Meccha Chameleon, R.E.P.O., the haystack game).
- **Overall volume:** 21,382 Steam releases in 2025, and 13,359 by mid-July 2026.

## F. Patterns that seem underused

These are my inferences from the gaps above, not claims from a source.

- **Factory/automation combined with friendslop chaos and clip-ready physics.** Factory games sell (StarRupture, Shapez 2), and co-op factory party games exist: HAM (2026-03-03), The Artifactory, Ready Steady Ship. None of them broke out. This is the closest gap to your factory-simulator idea.
- **Mic/voice as the core input.** Only three games broke out with it: Mage Arena, YAPYAP, Mimesis.
- **Player creativity as the main verb, the way painting is in Meccha Chameleon.** The follow-ups have been Roblox clones, not Steam games.
- **Episodic or appointment drops for indies,** as Dispatch did.
- **A free day-one giveaway,** as Content Warning did.
- **Satisfying-clean or absurd-scale progression (dig → sell → upgrade, the haystack loop) combined with co-op physics.** Demand is proven, but copying the haystack game itself now means competing with 10+ clones.
