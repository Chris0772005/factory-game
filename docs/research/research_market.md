## 1. Volume and median outcome
- **2025 releases:** 20,282 games were released on Steam. 608 reached 1,000+ reviews, 579 of them AA or indie. That is about 3%, the highest share of any year. Zukowski reads this as "we might be in a bit of an indie golden age." https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/01/08/how-many-games-were-released-in-2025/ , https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/01/27/what-the-hell-happened-in-2025/ , https://www.gamesradar.com/games/a-terrifying-20-282-games-were-released-on-steam-in-2025-and-just-608-managed-to-get-1-000-reviews-expert-finds-we-might-be-in-a-bit-of-an-indie-golden-age/
- **Other 2025 counts conflict:**
  - 21,344 games: UNVERIFIED, source unclear.
  - "19,000+, nearly half got under 10 reviews; Steam $16B+": headline only. https://80.lv/articles/steam-earned-usd16b-in-2025-but-nearly-half-of-19-000-games-got-under-10-reviews
- **Q1 2026:** for every game with 1,000+ reviews, 38 games had 0–9 reviews. https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/05/14/2026-q1-games/
- **Median revenue:** UNVERIFIED. Figures conflict wildly: $249 (source unclear), $1,728 (headline only, https://mezha.ua/en/news/steam-games-statistics-315726/), and "$5K–15K" (SEO site). The one well-supported conclusion is that the typical release makes almost nothing; about half get fewer than 10 reviews.
- **Concentration:** "Top 1% = 84.6% of revenue" and "~300 titles over $1M" are UNVERIFIED.

## 3. Genre by genre for small teams

**Incremental / idle: strong**
- Cast n Chill (June 2025): about $6.8M and 550k+ copies.
- The Farmer Was Replaced (1.0 in Oct 2025): $6.8M+ and 960k+ copies.
- CloverPit: $15.5M+. The source says "since May 2025"; I did not verify the release date.
- IdleOn (free-to-play): about $59M.

  All four: https://appmagic.rocks/research/idle-steam-games-2026
- A Game About Digging A Hole: released 7 Feb 2025 at €4.99, built in 3–4 weeks. It sold 250k copies in week 1 and passed 1M. https://www.gamesmarket.global/games-and-products/viral-hit-a-game-about-digging-a-hole-tops-one-million-sales-82fed80996941736a1a54c31381ab20b , https://wnhub.io/news/finance/item-47099

**Factory / automation: proven, but the top is held by big, deep games**
- The leaders are Factorio, Satisfactory, Dyson Sphere Program and Shapez 2. I found no hard data on saturation.
- Shapez 2:
  - 150k copies in 3 days at its Aug 2024 Early Access launch, and 260k+ by late August. https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-shapez-2-surged-to-250k-copies , https://www.pcgamesn.com/shapez-2/sales-milestone-reached
  - 1.0 in April 2026, with 650k+ copies sold since Early Access. Price was $20–25 in Early Access and $30 at 1.0. https://www.gamesmarket.global/tobspr-games-fully-launches-shapez-2-in-april/
  - Revenue estimates are UNVERIFIED and conflict: Raijin says 312k copies / $5.6M, VaporLens says 893k / $26.8M.
- Sandustry (a 2D pixel-physics factory game): 100k+ sales about a week after its Aug 2026 launch. https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/08/the-impressive-sandustry-already-hit-100k-sales-with-an-early-multiplayer-mod-out-now/ A claim of "200k+" is UNVERIFIED.
- My inference: small teams win here with a sharp twist (abstract shapes, pixel physics, programming), not by going head to head with Factorio.

**Simulator: strong (#2 genre by 1,000+ review hits in 2025; shop sims called out as "Great Conjunction")**
- Needle In A Haystack Simulator is the game the user means: Studio Bitdot, published by Polden Publishing, due Q4 2026.
  - Its clip got 40M+ views on X in a weekend, later 50M+.
  - Its August 2026 playtest drew about 23k players.
  - 8–10+ clones appeared within days. At least two released before the original; "A Needle In A Haystack: Sorting Game" came out on Sept 25.
  - PC Gamer calls the trend "haystack slop."
  
  https://kotaku.com/the-proliferation-of-copycats-on-steam-makes-discovering-original-games-like-finding-a-needle-in-a-haystack-2000734794 , https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/steam-week-in-review-great-haystack-slop-is-a-thing-now/ , https://en.univers-simu.com/news/needle-in-a-haystack-simulator-copies-steam-129698/ , https://store.steampowered.com/app/5159870/Needle_In_A_Haystack_Simulator/
- **Conclusion: this concept is already flooded with clones. Do not copy it; borrow the mechanic (manual sifting, then sell finds, then upgrade to automation) and use a different, fresh theme.**

**Co-op / friendslop: hot, high volume, Zukowski says NOT saturated (Jul 30 2026)**
- Concurrent players across friendslop games reached 366k in June 2026, when Meccha Chameleon launched.
- Each launch hit takes more than 50% of that audience, then drops to about 10% within 3–9 months; players rotate between games.
- Mage Arena, with an 11.75% share, still sold 624k copies / $3.69M in its debut week.

  https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/07/30/is-friendslop-saturated/ , https://gameworldobserver.com/2026/08/03/analyst-there-are-many-games-in-the-friendslap-niche-but-it-is-not-oversaturated
- R.E.P.O., PEAK and Schedule I: about 42M units / $377.5M combined. UNVERIFIED (https://voxbooster.com/blog/indie-game-statistics-2026/).

## 6. Saturated vs. underserved (my synthesis)
- **Avoid:**
  - Needle/haystack clones.
  - Generic cozy games.
  - AI-art games: low-wishlist games were punished at the June 2026 Next Fest, and GameDiscoverCo asks whether some genres reject AI (https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/are-steam-players-of-particular-genres).
  - Competing directly with Factorio or Satisfactory.
- **Best fit for a small team:**
  - An incremental hybrid with a physical, visual and clip-friendly hook, at a low price of about €5–10. Examples: Digging a Hole, The Farmer Was Replaced, Cast n Chill.
  - Or a factory/automation game with one novel twist, like Sandustry or Shapez 2.
  - Optional co-op ("friendslop") to reach the rotating audience of 366k concurrent players.
  - Simulation and incremental are both top-5 genres by 1,000+ review hits.
- **Price sweet spot:** not verified. The only data points are €4.99 for Digging a Hole and $20–30 for Shapez 2.
