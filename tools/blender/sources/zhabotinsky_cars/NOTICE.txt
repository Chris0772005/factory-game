Three cars with invented makes by Daniel Zhabotinsky (https://sketchfab.com/DanielZhabotinsky), each licensed
under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); every folder keeps the license.txt that Sketchfab
ships with the model:

- kiri86: "Kiri '86 - Low poly model",
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/kiri-86-low-poly-model-5c17c582ef9549798f694b660feea42a
- fairheaven_lt80: "Fairheaven LT '80 - Low poly model",
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fairheaven-lt-80-low-poly-model-e2678da920cc4be68dbc193727919ffb
- fairheaven_sw84: "Fairheaven SW '84 - Low poly model",
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fairheaven-sw-84-low-poly-model-aa0becb6e854422596cac6b21bf79787

Where these files come from: not from Sketchfab directly, but from the prepared copies in the GitHub repository
raisimTech/raisim2Lib, folder rsc/city/cars/, at commit d9df2c979c9ddc44ec6b6727ff72c660688fdb62 (license.txt,
model.gltf, model.bin and model_paint1.gltf of each car are byte-identical to the files there; checked
2026-11-19). The folders are unchanged copies. ATTRIBUTION_raisim_city.md is that repository's own notice
(rsc/city/ATTRIBUTION.md): it says what its authors changed against the Sketchfab downloads (transforms baked into
one mesh per car, maps reduced to 1K, the body paint replaced by a plain colour, two more paint colours added).

tools/blender/cars.py makes game/assets/models/vehicles/car_*.glb from these folders. It leaves out the cars'
badges (the atlas Carbadges_misc_U holds invented marks, some of which resemble real ones).
